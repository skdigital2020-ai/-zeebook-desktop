## 🌟 Why Use the Desktop App?

While ZeeBook is accessible from any web browser, the **Windows Desktop Edition** provides hardware-accelerated performance and direct hardware integrations designed specifically for studio counters:

* **⚡ Ultra-Fast Walk-in Billing**: Counter POS engineered to bill passport photos, album prints, and frames in under 3 seconds with global keyboard shortcuts (`Ctrl + Enter`).
* **🖨️ Native Thermal Printer Integration**: Direct plug-and-play USB support for standard 80mm and 58mm thermal receipt POS printers without print dialog delays.
* **📅 Shoot & Wedding Booking Calendar**: Full monthly visual calendar to schedule multi-day wedding shoots, indoor portrait sessions, and assign photographer crew without clash risks.
* **📦 Photo Lab & Album Delivery Pipeline**: Track customer album designs, third-party printing lab dispatches, and final customer delivery milestones.
* **🧾 Indian GST Invoicing & WhatsApp Sharing**: Compliant GST invoices with CGST/SGST splits, dynamic UPI payment QR codes, and 1-click WhatsApp bill sharing.
* **👥 Team & Freelancer Commission Tracking**: Manage lead photographers, drone pilots, video editors, and calculate per-event commission payouts.

## 🚀 Installation Guide

1. **Download the Installer**:
   Download the latest [**`ZeeBook-Setup.exe`**](https://github.com/skdigital2020-ai/ZeeBook/releases/latest) installer from the Releases section.

2. **Run the Installer**:
   Double-click `ZeeBook-Setup.exe` to begin installation.

3. **Windows SmartScreen Notice (First-time install)**:
   Because this is a freshly published application, Windows Defender SmartScreen may display:
   > *"Windows protected your PC"*
   
   * Click **More info**
   * Click **Run anyway**
   
   *(This notice appears only during the initial setup for newly compiled desktop software).*

4. **Launch & Sign In**:
   * ZeeBook will automatically install and create a desktop shortcut.
   * Open the app, enter your studio email and password, and begin managing your studio!

## ❓ Troubleshooting & FAQs

#### Q: How do I configure my thermal receipt printer?
Navigate to **Studio Settings ➔ Printer Settings** in the app. Select your connected USB thermal printer (58mm or 80mm) to enable 1-click instant receipt printing.

#### Q: Can I use ZeeBook on multiple counter computers?
Yes! ZeeBook uses real-time cloud sync. You can install ZeeBook on multiple counter PCs, laptops, and mobile devices—all data syncs instantly across your team.

#### Q: How do I receive future updates?
When an update is released, you will receive a notification within the app, or you can download the latest installer directly from this repository.
